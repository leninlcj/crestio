## 2026-04-05 — On not building features no one asked for

I shipped a "team analytics dashboard" in March. Spent a week on it. Five tutors clicked it once. I removed it last week.

Here's what I'm learning about feature requests: tutors will tell you what they wish existed. They will not tell you what they will use. The polish queue — the thing that genuinely changes a Sunday — was not in any feature request. The team analytics dashboard was. The first one shipped because I watched Sarah (HSC English, Sydney) try to write parent updates on a Sunday afternoon. The second one shipped because a tutoring centre owner asked for it on a sales call.

So I'm trying a new rule: ship things I've watched a real person need, not things I've heard a different person ask for. The bias is towards what's already broken in someone's actual day.

Phase 6 is mostly that. The interactive sandbox is here because a tutor told me she didn't want to "sign up just to see if it's worth it" — fair. Comparison pages are here because tutors keep asking "is this just TeachWorks but cheaper?" — answering it directly is more honest than handwaving. Customer stories are here because the homepage testimonial isn't enough and people aren't dumb.

I'm trying to build the kind of tool where the marketing matches the product. Both should feel like a peer.

— Lenin

## 2026-03-12 — A small team in Australia

People sometimes assume Crestio is a venture-backed thing. It isn't. It's me, a contractor in Melbourne who helps with frontend, and an accountant who keeps me out of trouble. We're profitable, we ship every two weeks or so, and we're growing through tutors telling other tutors.

I bring this up because a few tutors have asked, gently, whether Crestio is going to disappear next year — they got burned by a previous tool that shut down with two months' notice. I understand the worry. Here's what I can promise: I am not flipping this. There's no exit strategy. The 1% platform fee on parent payments was deliberately designed so that even with a few hundred tutors we cover hosting and Anthropic API costs without needing a billion-dollar exit.

If Crestio ever needs to wind down, you'll get 12 months notice and a full export of every byte of your data — not just CSV, the actual files and PDFs too. That's in the terms. It's not legal cover, it's a commitment.

— Lenin

## 2026-02-08 — What 14F was actually about

We shipped Stripe Connect for parent payments two weeks ago. On the surface: parents now pay by card, money lands in the tutor's bank in 2 days, we take 1%. Done.

What it actually was: the part of running a tutoring practice that scared tutors the most was the money. The bank transfer that arrived 4 days late. The parent who said "I'll pay you Friday" and didn't. The week you needed the money and chased every household.

When you remove that anxiety, what comes back is the time you spent worrying about it. Tutors I've talked to say the same thing: "I don't think about money anymore." That's the line I'm chasing. Not "easier billing." Not "automated finance." Just — you stop thinking about it. You teach.

The next thing is auto-pay. A parent saves a card; new invoices charge automatically. The dashboard becomes a place you visit because you want to, not because you have to.

— Lenin
