## title: Voice-to-session
status: in_progress
audience: tutor
eta: 2026-Q2
description: Press-and-hold to record after a lesson — the recording becomes a draft session with notes, ready to polish. Trade 8 seconds of typing for 8 seconds of talking.

## title: Personalized AI polish
status: in_progress
audience: tutor
eta: 2026-Q2
description: After 20 polished notes, polish learns your voice — phrases you use, what you call your students, how long your typical update is. Less editing, more shipping.

## title: Google Calendar sync
status: in_progress
audience: tutor
eta: 2026-Q2
description: Two-way sync. New sessions in Crestio show up on Google Calendar with the parent's name and subject. Reschedules in either direction stay in lockstep.

## title: Apple Calendar sync
status: planned
audience: tutor
eta: 2026-Q3
description: Same two-way sync as Google, via CalDAV. Native on iOS, no extra app.

## title: SMS notifications for parents
status: planned
audience: parent
eta: 2026-Q3
description: Send polished notes by SMS for parents who never check email. Tutor opts in per-parent. We absorb the SMS cost on annual plans.

## title: Public API
status: planned
audience: infra
eta: 2026-Q4
description: REST API for sessions, students, invoices. Webhook events. For tutors who want to wire Crestio into their existing tools — Zapier, Make, custom dashboards.

## title: Term reports as branded PDFs
status: in_progress
audience: parent
eta: 2026-Q2
description: Quarterly PDF for each student, auto-compiled from your polished notes. Tutor's branding, downloadable from the parent portal.

## title: Parent satisfaction signal
status: in_progress
audience: tutor
eta: 2026-Q2
description: Parents thumb-up or thumb-down each polished note. Tutor sees the aggregate per student, never individual ratings. Helps you spot the parent who is quietly drifting.

## title: Auto-pay invoices
status: in_progress
audience: parent
eta: 2026-Q2
description: Parents save a card and turn on auto-pay. New invoices charge automatically. No more "did you see my invoice?" follow-ups.

## title: Mobile app (iOS / Android)
status: planned
audience: tutor
eta: 2026-Q4
description: Native shell around the existing web app, plus push notifications. The web product already works on phones — this adds the home-screen icon and the alerts.

## title: Tutor referral program
status: shipped
audience: tutor
eta: 2026-Q2
description: Send a friend Crestio. Both of you get a free month after they pay for the first time.

## title: Customer success — your impact
status: shipped
audience: tutor
eta: 2026-Q2
description: Daily rotating insight on the dashboard. "You sent 12 polished notes this week — that's 4 hours of explaining you didn't have to do."
